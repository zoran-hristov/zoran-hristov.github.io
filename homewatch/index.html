HomeWatch

HomeWatch is a personal, non-commercial home-monitoring application, written and run by an individual developer for use in a single household. It is not a product or a service, and it is not available to the public.

What it does

  • Shows which devices on the home network are active and which internet services they contact, and flags unusual activity.
  • Takes still images from the household's own cameras and produces short text descriptions of them.
  • Keeps backup copies of those images, and of its own database, in the developer's own Google Drive.

Google Drive access

HomeWatch uses the Google Drive API only to store and manage the backup files it creates itself, in the developer's own Drive account. It does not read or change any other files, and it does not share data from Google with anyone. See the privacy policy for details.
