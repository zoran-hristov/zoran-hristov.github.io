HomeWatch – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 10 October 2026

HomeWatch is a personal, non-commercial home-monitoring application written and operated by an individual developer ("I", "me") for use in a single household. It is not offered to the public, is not sold, and has no customers. This policy explains what data HomeWatch handles and how.

1. Who uses HomeWatch

HomeWatch is used only by its developer and members of the developer's own household. No one else can create an account or sign in.

2. Data from Google

HomeWatch connects to the developer's own Google Drive account through the Google Drive API, solely to store backup copies of files that HomeWatch itself creates (camera snapshots, short video clips, and database backups).

  • Access requested: permission to create and manage files that HomeWatch itself creates in Google Drive. HomeWatch does not read, modify, or delete any other files in the account.
  • Use: the data is used only to provide the backup feature described above.
  • Storage of credentials: the Google sign-in token is stored only on the HomeWatch device in the developer's home. It is never sent to any other party.
  • Sharing: data obtained from Google APIs is not sold, shared with, or transferred to any third party, is not used for advertising, and is not used to develop, improve, or train artificial intelligence or machine-learning models.
  • Human access: no person other than the developer reads this data.

HomeWatch's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

3. Other data HomeWatch processes

On the household's own device, HomeWatch records local network activity (which home devices contacted which internet domains) and images from the household's own cameras. Camera images may be sent to an AI image-analysis service chosen by the developer to produce short text descriptions. This data belongs to the household, stays on the home device or in the developer's own cloud storage, and is not sold or shared for any other purpose.

4. Retention and deletion

Data is kept on the home device for a limited period and in the developer's own Google Drive until the developer deletes it. Access for HomeWatch can be withdrawn at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

5. Cookies and tracking

This website and HomeWatch use no analytics, advertising, or tracking cookies.
